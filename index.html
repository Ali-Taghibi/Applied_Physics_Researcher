EXECUTIVE BIOGRAPHY & SCIENTIFIC TRAJECTORY

Scientific Journey From Subatomic Physics to Industrial Technologies and Clinical Impact

My academic journey began with a B.Sc. in Physics at Damghan University (2011–2015), followed by graduate research in Applied Physics at K. N. Toosi University of Technology (2015–2018). Throughout my career, my core scientific mission has been to bridge high-precision Monte Carlo particle transport codes (Geant4-DNA, MCNP6, MCNPX, and FLUKA) with urgent environmental and medical engineering challenges.

In environmental technology, I model the nanoscale radiolysis of water and wastewater under high-energy electron beams (EBWT/EBFGT)—showing how reactive species (•OH, e⁻_aq, H•) mineralize persistent trace micropollutants that conventional treatments fail to remove. In nuclear medicine and radiation protection, I have co-designed heavy-water (D₂O) hybrid targets for accelerator-based Molybdenum-99 (⁹⁹Mo) production, authored NCRP 151 labyrinth shielding and RPP/RPR frameworks, and coordinated interdisciplinary R&D on smart fiber-optic dosimetric fabrics.

1970s MILESTONE

Foundational Pulse Radiolysis & Boston Deer Island Sludge Pilots

Early empirical validation of high-energy electron beams for disinfecting municipal sewage sludge and degrading recalcitrant organic pollutants. Researchers established primary water radiolysis G-values, though depth-dose uniformity and multi-scavenger competition remained empirical approximations.

2- Evolution of Electron-Beam Flue Gas Treatment (1970s–Present)

Click on each historical milestone node below to explore plasma-chemical radical pathways, dry ammonium fertilizer recovery, and multi-pollutant (SO₂ / NOₓ / VOCs / Dioxins) abatement advances.

1970s MILESTONE

Discovery of Dry Simultaneous SO₂/NOₓ Radiolytic Oxidation & Ammonia Injection

Pioneering batch and bench-scale experiments by Ebara Corp. and JAERI (Japan) demonstrated that irradiating humid flue gas with high-energy electrons generates oxidizing radicals (•OH, O•, HO₂•) that rapidly convert SO₂ and NOₓ into H₂SO₄ and HNO₃, which react with stoichiometric NH₃ to form solid agricultural fertilizer aerosols.

Key Technical Parameters:
• Beam Energy: 0.3 – 0.75 MeV (Bench & Pilot)
• Mechanism: N₂/O₂/H₂O → •OH, O(³P), HO₂•
• Byproduct: Dry (NH₄)₂SO₄ & NH₄NO₃ Aerosol
